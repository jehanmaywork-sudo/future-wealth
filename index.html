A NOTE FROM JEHAN
I wanted a better relationship with money. I didn’t want another app that only showed me what I spent or made me feel bad when life got expensive. I wanted something that could help me take care of my bills, understand what I really have left, save for the future, still enjoy the present, and keep the things I dream about in sight.

I also wanted somewhere I could say, “This is what’s going on with my money,” even when my thoughts were messy — and get help turning that into a plan. That’s why I started building Future Wealth: not just to track money, but to help give it direction while building a life I’m excited about.

— Jehan
